Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. How grofolio.tech respects your privacy and handles data.

Introduction

At grofolio.tech, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected when you browse our website, how that data is used, and the steps we take to protect your information.

Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, login credentials, or personal identification to read our articles. We collect only standard browsing information required to operate, optimize, and serve content:

  • Server logs: Standard request logs including IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and access timestamps.
  • Local preferences: We use your browser's local storage (only with your explicit consent) to remember whether you prefer light mode or dark mode.

Cookies and Advertising Partners

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to grofolio.tech and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

Google Tag Manager and Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager to manage site tags and monitor site performance. Google Tag Manager does not collect personally identifiable information directly; it triggers scripts that analyze aggregate visitor traffic (such as popular articles and device formats) to help us improve guide clarity and page loading speeds.

Third-Party Links

Our blog posts and resources contain links to external sites (such as official documentation, industry research, and tools). We have no control over the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request access to or deletion of personal data. Since we do not store personal profiles, we collect only anonymous, aggregate telemetry.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our About page or email us at contact@grofolio.tech.
