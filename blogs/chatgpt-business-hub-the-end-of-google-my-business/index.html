ChatGPT Business Hub the End of Google My Business? What Local & Online Brands Need to Know

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Business Hub in beta to let businesses claim profiles, verify details, and track AI recommendations. Here is what it means for your brand and whether Google My Business is truly obsolete.

The direct answer: No, ChatGPT Business Hub is not the immediate death of Google My Business (Google Business Profile). Google Maps and local 3-pack search still dominate high-frequency immediate queries ("coffee shop near me", "emergency plumber"). However, ChatGPT Business Hub introduces a monumental paradigm shift: customer discovery is rapidly transitioning from keyword-based SERP browsing to conversational, AI-synthesized recommendations. Businesses that treat both ecosystems as essential will capture the next wave of high-intent buyers.

Introduction: How customer discovery is changing

For over two decades, the digital playbook for customer acquisition followed a predictable formula. Businesses built a website, registered a Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business), gathered 5-star reviews, optimized for local keywords, and fought for placement in Google's coveted local 3-pack and organic search snippets.

That playbook worked because when people had a problem, they opened Google, typed a search term, clicked through three or four blue links, and manually compared options.

Today, customer behavior is undergoing its biggest transformation since the smartphone. Instead of clicking back and forth across a dozen websites, prospective buyers are asking AI assistants to do the legwork for them:

"Which boutique digital marketing agency in Chicago has proven experience scaling B2B SaaS startups from $1M to $10M ARR?"

"Recommend a top-rated emergency dentist open on Sunday in North Austin that accepts Delta Dental and offers online booking."

Rather than delivering a raw list of hyperlinks, ChatGPT analyzes, filters, and recommends specific businesses directly inside the chat interface.

Recognizing that businesses need a structured way to manage their presence and understand how they are discovered within AI conversations, OpenAI quietly rolled out the beta of ChatGPT Business Hub on November 10, 2026.

So, what exactly is ChatGPT Business Hub, how does it compare to Google Business Profile, and what steps should business owners take right now?

What is ChatGPT Business Hub?

ChatGPT Business Hub is a centralized portal developed by OpenAI where verified organizations and business owners can claim their brand entity, control authoritative company facts, monitor brand mentions, and evaluate how ChatGPT recommends them to users.

In practical terms, it is OpenAI's answer to Google Business Profile (GBP). Where GBP serves as Google's official source of truth for Google Search and Google Maps, ChatGPT Business Hub serves as OpenAI's structured knowledge layer for ChatGPT Search and conversational answers.

Instead of relying exclusively on web scrapers and unstructured public crawls that can misinterpret outdated blog posts or stale contact information, the Business Hub allows authorized representatives to maintain verified data directly with the model.

The 5 core capabilities of ChatGPT Business Hub

OpenAI has structured the initial beta of Business Hub around five foundational pillars designed to bridge the gap between AI generation and real-world business operations:

1. Claim and verify your business entity

Similar to verifying ownership on Google Search Console or Google Business Profile, organizations can search for their existing entity in the Business Hub directory and complete a verification workflow.

Once verified, authorized brand representatives gain exclusive administrative control over the business's public profile within the OpenAI knowledge graph, preventing unauthorized edits or impersonation.

2. Maintain an authoritative source of truth

One of the biggest frustrations business owners have experienced with Large Language Models (LLMs) is hallucinated or outdated information—such as discontinued services, incorrect pricing tiers, or old operating addresses.

Business Hub allows you to update and pin critical details:

  • Official website URL and primary landing pages
  • Accurate business description and core value proposition
  • Catalog of offered services, products, and specialties
  • High-resolution official brand logo and media assets
  • Service areas, operating hours, and verified contact channels

When ChatGPT generates answers recommending your company or responding to direct queries about your offerings, it references these verified data attributes to ensure accuracy.

3. Track brand mentions and website click-throughs

Historically, tracking AI referrals has been notoriously difficult. Web analytics platforms often lumped AI visits into direct traffic or ambiguous referral buckets.

Business Hub introduces an analytics dashboard that reveals:

  • Mention frequency: How often your brand is cited or recommended in user conversations across ChatGPT.
  • Referral clicks: The exact volume of click-throughs generated from citation links in ChatGPT answers to your website.
  • Trend trajectories: Week-over-week and month-over-month visibility fluctuations.

4. Uncover customer discovery topics

Perhaps the most valuable feature for marketers is Discovery Topics. This report groups the recurring conversational themes, intent patterns, and problem statements that led ChatGPT to recommend or cite your brand.

For example, if you run a cybersecurity consulting firm, Discovery Topics might reveal that users frequently discover you when asking about "SOC 2 Type II compliance automation" or "HIPAA cloud architecture audit."

Strategic takeaway: Discovery Topics provide direct market intelligence. You can use these insights to double down on high-converting service pages, refine case studies, and craft content tailored to the exact questions buyers are asking AI assistants.

5. Connect with customers through AI Business Agents

In addition to static profile data, eligible businesses can deploy customized, conversational Business Agents.

Rather than forcing a customer to leave ChatGPT, visit a landing page, and wait 24 hours for a sales representative to reply, an authorized Business Agent can answer detailed product questions, check real-time service availability, qualify lead requirements, and book consultations directly inside the interaction flow.

Why local and online brands must pay attention right now

Dismissing ChatGPT Business Hub as "just another listing site" is a dangerous strategic mistake. Here is why both brick-and-mortar local businesses and digital online brands should take note:

1. The "Winner-Take-Most" nature of AI recommendations

In a classic Google search, a user scrolls through 10 organic results, sponsored ads, and the map pack. Even if you rank #5 or #6, you still capture a meaningful percentage of impressions and exploratory clicks.

AI search operates under radically different dynamics. When a user asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, the model does not list twenty companies—it typically synthesizes a tight response featuring only one to three select businesses, accompanied by reasons why they fit the user's specific criteria.

If your company has not claimed its profile, verified its core competencies, and earned strong entity authority in AI knowledge graphs, you are invisible in conversational search results.

2. The shift from Traditional SEO to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Traditional SEO focuses on keyword density, backlink quantity, meta tags, and URL hierarchies.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) focuses on entity clarity, semantic context, brand sentiment across digital publications, transparent pricing and service definitions, and verified first-party data. Claiming your ChatGPT Business Hub profile is the single most authoritative first-party signal you can feed directly into OpenAI's systems.

3. High purchasing intent of conversational queries

People using conversational AI to find business solutions are rarely casually browsing. They usually have complex, highly specific requirements and ready budgets:

  • "I need a commercial real estate broker in Denver who handles warehouse leases with rail access."
  • "Find a certified Shopify Plus agency with experience migrating enterprise Magento stores."

When ChatGPT recommends your company to an inquiry with that level of specificity, the resulting lead converts at a substantially higher rate than a generic keyword click.

How to claim and optimize your ChatGPT Business Hub profile

Taking control of your entity on ChatGPT Business Hub takes less than thirty minutes. Follow this systematic setup checklist:

  1. Access the Business Hub Portal: Navigate to the official OpenAI Business Hub directory (chatgpt.com/business-hub) while logged into your primary organization account.
  2. Search for Your Business Entity: Type your exact registered company name into the entity search bar. Because OpenAI crawls public corporate registries, Wikipedia, LinkedIn, and major business directories, your entity may already exist in an unclaimed state.
  3. Initiate Verification: Click Claim Profile. Verification is conducted via corporate domain email matching (e.g. @yourcompany.com), DNS TXT record authentication, or official identity documents.
  4. Complete Your Core Knowledge Profile:
    • Input your exact legal and trade name.
    • Provide a concise, jargon-free 150-word description highlighting who you serve, what problems you solve, and what sets you apart.
    • List your active service offerings and proprietary methodologies using clear, standardized terminology.
    • Upload your official high-resolution logo (square 512x512 PNG with solid or transparent background).
    • Add your verified physical locations, service radiuses, and primary customer support URLs.
  5. Audit Discovery Topics Weekly: Once your profile is active, check the Discovery Topics tab every Monday to observe which buyer prompts generate brand impressions and where prospective customers are clicking.

The Verdict: Is Google My Business dead?

Let's be completely unequivocal: Google My Business (Google Business Profile) is not dead, nor will it vanish anytime soon.

If a commuter's tire blows out on the interstate, they are not having a 10-turn philosophical conversation with an AI model. They open Google Maps, search "tow truck near me", tap the first profile with 4.8 stars and 200 reviews, and hit the "Call" button. Google still commands over 85% of total global search volume, and Google Maps remains the undisputed utility for hyper-local foot traffic.

However, viewing this as an "either/or" scenario is a false dilemma.

The smart strategy is a Dual-Visibility Engine:

  • Use Google Business Profile to capture local proximity traffic, map directions, immediate phone calls, and high-frequency local reviews.
  • Use ChatGPT Business Hub to capture advisory, high-consideration B2B inquiries, complex service evaluations, and the booming demographic of users who conduct all product research inside conversational AI.

Actionable 7-day checklist for marketing teams

To ensure your business leads the curve rather than playing catch-up, execute these five tasks over the next seven days:

  1. Day 1-2: Search and claim your official entity on ChatGPT Business Hub. Verify domain ownership.
  2. Day 3: Audit your business descriptions across your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and ChatGPT Business Hub. Ensure services, locations, and value propositions are perfectly synchronized.
  3. Day 4: Check your website's Schema.org structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, Service markup) to make sure AI web scrapers can parse your entities without friction.
  4. Day 5: Run test queries in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity for your primary commercial offerings. Take note of which competitors are recommended and why the model cites them.
  5. Day 6-7: Document the gaps identified in AI test queries and publish clear, authoritative case studies and FAQs on your website addressing those specific decision factors.

Conclusion

Customer discovery has never remained static. Just as businesses had to adapt from the Yellow Pages to Google Search in the 2000s, and from desktop websites to mobile-first profiles in the 2010s, modern businesses must now adapt to conversational AI discovery in the 2020s.

Google My Business remains an essential foundation of local marketing, but ChatGPT Business Hub represents the first major milestone of AI-era brand governance. Claiming your presence today secures your visibility for tomorrow's buyers.
